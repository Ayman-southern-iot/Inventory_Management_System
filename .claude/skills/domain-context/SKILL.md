# Domain essentials

Compressed reference. For detail, open the one relevant file in `docs/reference/`.

## Stock

- **Product** = the catalogue card, one stable ID forever.
- **Placement** = product × compartment × quantity. A product with stock in two compartments has
  two placement rows. This is what makes "move 30 of 70" work without changing the product ID.
- **Location** is two levels: Zone ("Meta", "Nvidia") → Compartment ("1A", "3C").
- `available = quantity − reserved_qty`. Borrow requests reserve; issuing decrements both;
  returns increment quantity. Consumables are issued and never return.
- The **ledger** is append-only and authoritative. Placement quantities are a cache of it.
- Only `StockService` writes stock. Always `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`, always ordered by id.

## Roles

`GENERAL`, `APPROVER`, `INVENTORY_MANAGER` are assignable by an `ADMIN`. Roles are **additive** —
everyone can borrow and raise requisitions; an approver is a general user with approval rights.
A user holds a *set* of roles.

## Requisition lifecycle

`DRAFT → IM_REVIEW → AWAITING_APPROVAL → APPROVED → BOM_GENERATED → SENT_TO_ACCOUNTS →
FUNDS_PARTIAL/FUNDS_RECEIVED → PURCHASED → STOCKED → CLOSED`, with `REJECTED` terminal from any
approval stage.

- The Inventory Manager approves **first** — their approval means "confirmed, we don't have this".
- Then approvers act **in parallel**, no fixed order. Above the expense threshold there are two.
- **Any single rejection kills the whole request.** It does not need both.
- An approver may **withdraw** their approval until the BOM is generated.
- Approver count is **frozen at submit** from the then-current threshold. Changing the threshold
  never reshuffles in-flight requisitions.

## The three money figures

- **Requested** — the requester's estimate, frozen at submit.
- **Approved** — the sanctioned figure, defaults to requested, may be revised down at approval.
- **Funded** — the running sum of `fund_receipts`. Partial funding is normal.

The Inventory Manager is **never** notified when a remaining balance arrives; they check manually.

## BOM

Generated by the IM after full approval, from one *or more* approved requisitions. Carries an
immutable snapshot of approver names and designations — never rendered by joining live to `users`,
because a July BOM must still show July's job titles.

## Vocabulary

**IM** = Inventory Manager · **BOM** = Bill of Materials · **BDT** = Bangladeshi Taka ·
**Placement** = stock of one product in one compartment · **Footprint** = an approval signature
block on the BOM.
